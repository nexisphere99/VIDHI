Wrap this around every character prompt to get a clean reference-sheet style image:

### PREFIX (add BEFORE character details):
```
Full-body front-facing character reference sheet. Standing pose, arms relaxed at sides or one hand on hip, weight evenly distributed on both feet. Neutral solid background (light grey #E0E0E0 or white). Full body visible from top of head to feet, centered in frame. Studio lighting   soft even light from front, minimal shadows. Camera angle: straight-on, eye level. Character looking directly at viewer. High detail, photorealistic, full resolution. Indian character design reference.
```

### SUFFIX (add AFTER character details):
```
Full body in frame with 10% margin on all sides. No cropping. Feet visible touching ground plane. Subtle drop shadow beneath feet for grounding. Clean reference sheet style   no background elements, no props unless specified in clothing. Aspect ratio 2:3 portrait orientation. Photorealistic skin texture, natural lighting, no filters, no beautification apps aesthetic.
```

