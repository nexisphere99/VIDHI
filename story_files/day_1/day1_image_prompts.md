## STYLE GUIDE NOTES

- **Consistent character designs** across all images   same face, same proportions, same coloring
- **Indian-specific details matter**: correct salwar kameez draping, proper bindi/tilak placement, accurate food presentation, realistic hostel/PG architecture
- **Lighting is emotional**: warm golden for comfort/home scenes, cool blue-grey for loneliness/hostel mornings, amber-mystical for the swap ritual
- **No explicit nudity** in generated images   intimate scenes use suggestion, shadow, strategic framing
- **Cultural accuracy**: Pune-specific architecture (colonial + modern mix), correct vehicle types (Pulsar, Activa, auto-rickshaws), appropriate signage in Marathi/Hindi
- **Avoid AI tells**: no six-fingered hands, no melted text, no inconsistent shadows
