## STYLE CONTINUITY NOTES   DAY 2

- **All Day 1 character designs remain identical.** Same faces, same proportions, same coloring. The only changes are clothing and expression.
- **Day 2 lighting shifts toward WARMER tones**   hope is entering the story. Day 1 was dominated by cool, melancholy blues. Day 2 adds golden hour warmth (temple, evening preparation, the planning session).
- **The TEMPLE is key**   Pataleshwar appears in evening golden light today (scouting visit). It will appear in pre-dawn blue-grey tomorrow (Day 3, the swap). Establish the location now so the contrast hits on Day 3.
- **TENSION images** (Mrs. Joshi rounds, Priya suspicious) should use slightly desaturated tones   the color draining when threat approaches.
- **The intimate scenes** (gym body awareness, night anticipation, body catalogue) use WARM CLOSE tones   amber, gold, moonlight. The body is being honored, not sexualized. The images should feel like portraiture, not voyeurism.
- **Phone screens** should be consistent with Day 1 UI mockups   same apps, same themes, updated content.
