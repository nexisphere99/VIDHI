## STYLE NOTES — DAY 3

- **THE KEY VISUAL CHALLENGE**: showing the WRONG expression on the RIGHT face. Kavya's face with Arjun's emotions. Arjun's face with Kavya's intelligence. The body is correct; the soul behind the eyes is not. This dissonance IS the visual story.
- **Expression artists**: study how the same face changes when different personalities inhabit it. The jaw set, the eye focus, the tension in the brow — these micro-expressions tell the viewer that the person inside doesn't match the person outside.
- **The SWAP scene** should feel mythic — ancient stone, red kumkum, pre-dawn blue. This is a sacred act in a sacred space.
- **The CODING scene** should feel intimate — a person crying at a keyboard is not sad, it's beautiful. The tears are joy. The light is warm. The code on screen is the most beautiful thing in the image.
- **The REUNION** should be devastating — two women's bodies holding a man's love. The viewer should feel the six months of separation in the grip of the embrace.
- **Body scenes** (first check, bathroom) are NOT depicted visually for safety — they exist in text only. The images capture the EMOTIONAL moments, not the physical discovery.
