## STYLE NOTES — DAY 4

- **WARMER palette across the board.** Day 4 is about comfort growing — the images should reflect this shift from Day 3's sharp contrasts to Day 4's settling warmth. More amber, more gold, more the color of things becoming familiar.
- **The BRA VICTORY image** should feel like an achievement — small, domestic, and genuinely triumphant. Think of the visual language of milestone moments in sports films, applied to a bra hook.
- **The MEERA NIGHT scene** is the most tender image in the entire Day 4 set. Two women's faces close together, amber light, stolen time. The warmth should be overwhelming. The viewer should feel the six months of waiting in the closeness.
- **The BODY EXPLORATION image** (Kavya at the mirror) should be SCIENTIFIC, not sexual. The gaze in the mirror is the gaze of a researcher, not a lover. Curiosity, not desire.
- **The AAI VIDEO CALL** should capture the EMOTIONAL CONTRAST — a face performing "son" while the soul behind it discovers what unconditional love feels like. Bittersweet without being maudlin.
- **GYM DISASTER** is comedy — play it for humor. The wobbling bar, Rohit's alarm, the physical comedy of a brain that doesn't match the body's capabilities.
