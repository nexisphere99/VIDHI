## STYLE NOTES — DAY 4 (REVISED)

- **THREE LIGHT PHASES:** Pre-dawn stealth (near-black, grey highlights, the visual of covert ops), daytime warmth (golden lecture halls, warm hostel rooms), golden-hour swap-back (the temple in sunset light, the basalt glowing).
- **The SNEAKING OUT image** should be COMEDY — the Maggi tower obstacle, the gym bag tripwire, the sleeping Rohit as an unconscious sentry. Think heist-movie framing applied to a 21-year-old leaving his own room.
- **The MEERA HOLDING image** is the emotional centerpiece. The embrace should feel TOTAL — six months compressed into physical contact. Meera's hand placement is KEY: against the outer ribcage, near-but-not-on the breast. The proximity IS the story.
- **The TEMPLE SWAP-BACK** bookends the day — the same location in different light. Dawn (blue-grey, anticipation) vs. sunset (golden, reflection). The visual rhyme connects beginning and end.
- **The ERECTION ALARM expression** must read as FEMININE bewilderment in MASCULINE features — the disconnect between the face's gender and the expression's origin is the visual punch.
